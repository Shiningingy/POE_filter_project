Drop-restricted item

From Path of Exile Wiki
(Redirected from Drop disabled)
Jump to navigation Jump to search

"Drop-restricted" is unofficial terminology used for items that are unobtainable in normal gameplay due to special requirements such as:

  • specific Unique Monsters or Bosses
  • monsters that only appear as part of a league mechanic (e.g. Breach Uniques and rings)
  • interactions with a specific league mechanic
  • deprecated or obsolete game mechanics

Unique items with drop restrictions cannot be obtained using Orbs of Chance, Ancient Orbs or Tainted Mythic Orbs.

Race rewards

Demigod uniques are a special class of items given as rewards for the winners of those races. To acquire the basetype of a Demigod unique, players would need to corrupt 5 Demigod unique items into corrupted rare items, then use the 5-rare-to-1-rare vendor recipe to obtain an unidentified rare item that has the base type of the Demigod unique.

Items introduced in challenge leagues

See also: league-specific item

Temporary challenge leagues often introduce new items that are drop-restricted to that league's mechanic. For example, during Scourge league, Tainted Chromatic Orbs could only drop from the Scourge league mechanic.

Some league mechanics are later incorporated into the core game and have a default non-zero chance to spawn, while others are not and require either atlas passives, scarabs, or map crafts to spawn. Some league mechanics are completely unable to spawn but the items introduced in those leagues still exist in Standard league. When a league mechanic is added to the core game, it can usually drop items that were drop-restricted to that mechanic when it was a temporary league. For example, when Scourge was added to replace Beyond, Tainted Chromatic Orbs could be obtained once again.

When unique items from a league are reintroduced to the core game, some of them are added to the core drop pool of items that can drop anywhere and that can be chanced.

The following items were introduced in leagues that did not become part of the core game, but remain readily obtainable:

  • Scarabs are part of the core drop pool and are also available as Immortal Syndicate safehouse rewards.

Legacy

Legacy items

See also: List of base items with legacy variants and List of unique items with legacy variants

Legacy items are discontinued versions of items that have been subjected to balance changes which do not affect existing copies of the item. Due to their limited availability, their prices can be high in Standard league.

Most legacy items can be updated to their newest stats by using a Divine Orb or Blessed Orb on it, to create a pseudo legacy variant that may have a combination of legacy mods and newer, better mod value ranges (or in some case, worse mod range). These orbs won't work when the base item was changed, either. Thus, remember to use advanced mod description (default PC keybind: Alt) to check which pseudo legacy variants can be created from using a Divine Orb or Blessed Orb by viewing the current mod value range.

Sacred Orb can also improve legacy items: for example a Queen of the Forest legacy variant predates the introduction of Sacred Orbs.

Pseudo legacy variants

It is possible, although generally not desirable, to craft pseudo legacy variants of certain unique items by chancing legacy base items. A unique item created this way will retain the legacy base item's implicit modifier and have the current explicit modifiers.

Legacy names

See also: List of items with legacy names

Certain unique items have had their names changed. Those items that were obtained before the name change was implemented will forever retain the old name.

Legacy modifiers

See also: List of legacy modifiers

Some item affixes were changed or removed from the drop pool altogether for balance reasons.

Drop-disabled items

See also: List of drop-disabled items

Certain items may be removed from the game entirely. In other cases, existing items may remain but new copies cannot be obtained in the current game version. In the latter case, these items can only be obtained by trading with other players in a permanent league.

This category also encompasses items that could never be obtained by the player directly in the first place, but could be interacted with as part of a mechanic, such as the Djinn-Touched Vaal Orb.

Event Items

Event Rewards

Main article: List of alternate art items

Each race and player-versus-player season offers rewards of existing items with alternate art for reaching certain point breakpoints within the season. Event-specific rewards (and alternate art counterparts of these) are also offered for placing well in the race or the season. All rewards are placed into remove-only stash tabs that can be viewed from any league, but once removed, they remain in the league they were taken into and cannot be transferred to other leagues. These rewards cannot be viewed or removed from the stash in temporary leagues until four weeks have passed since the beginning of the league.

Event Prizes

Main article: Event prize

At the end of each event and at the end of the season, unique trophy items are distributed among the best race and pvp participants. They follow the same rules as Event Rewards.

Demigod Uniques are considered Event Prizes.
